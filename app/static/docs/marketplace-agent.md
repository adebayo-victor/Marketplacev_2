## 1. What Marketplace Is

Marketplace is a web platform where a merchant opens one or more kiosks (online stores). Each kiosk gets its own public link, a product catalog, and a WhatsApp-based checkout.

What you get as a merchant:

| Feature | What it does for you |
| --- | --- |
| AI storefront builder | Pick a category, describe your vibe, and the AI designs a store that matches your business. |
| WhatsApp ordering | Customers pick items and options, then check out. You receive a clean, itemized order in WhatsApp. |
| 0% commission | The platform takes no cut of your actual sales. |
| Flyer Studio | Turn any product into a social-media flyer (PNG or PDF) in a few taps. |
| Invoices and receipts | Send a payment-request invoice, then switch to a "PAID" receipt once money arrives. |
| Made-to-order stock mode | Items such as cooked food, cakes, services or digital goods never show "out of stock". |
| Lost-lead recovery | See customers who started checkout but never sent the WhatsApp message, and nudge them. |
| Sponsor ad slots | Sell banner space on your store to earn extra money. |
| Wallet and coins | Top up with Naira (₦1 = 1 Coin), earn coins by referring friends, and spend coins on platform rewards. |
| Monthly Subscription | Keep your kiosk live for ₦5,000 per month. Early renewals add 30 days to your current expiry. |
| Referrals | Invite other merchants and earn coins when they join. |

## 4. Quick Start for Merchants

1. **Create your account.** Sign up with a username, email and password, or tap Continue with Google.
2. **Open a kiosk.** From your Hub, tap **+ OPEN A NEW KIOSK**.
3. **Choose a category.** This guides the AI's design style.
4. **Name your kiosk and set your link.** The link fills in automatically from the name, and you can edit it.
5. **Enter your WhatsApp number.** Orders are sent here.
6. **Describe your dream look** in the AI Design Instructions box. Upload a logo, banner or background if you have them.
7. **Tap LAUNCH STOREFRONT.** The AI builds your store in the background. Your kiosk shows BUILDING and unlocks by itself when ready.
8. **Add products** with photos, prices and options.
9. **Preview your store** on the phone-style preview to check it looks right.
10. **Activate your monthly subscription (₦5,000/month)** when you're happy, or use coins if you have enough.
11. **Share your link** on your WhatsApp Status, Instagram bio and anywhere customers find you.

*Good to know: You can build, redesign, add products and test ordering in Preview mode before paying anything.*

## 8. Coins, Payments, and the Monthly Subscription

### Ways to keep your kiosk live (Monthly Subscription)
*   **Pay ₦5,000 from your Hub** using the **Activate / Renew (₦5,000/month) →** button. Payment is handled by Paystack. After paying, your kiosk is activated for exactly 30 days.
*   **Early Renewal:** If you renew before your current 30 days are up, the new 30 days are simply added to your current expiration date.
*   **Expiry:** If the 30 days pass without renewal, the kiosk is automatically locked and visitors will see the "Landlord Don Put Padlock!" page until you pay again.
*   **Redeem coins** for the kiosk-activation reward on the Finance page, if you have enough.

### Direct Wallet Top-Ups
*   You can purchase coins directly through the platform using Paystack.
*   The exchange rate is strictly **1 Naira = 1 Coin**.
*   Payments are verified securely on the server. You do not need to do anything extra; coins are instantly credited to your wallet upon successful verification.

### Admin Balance Overrides
*   Platform admins have a secure panel to manually add or remove coins from a user's wallet (e.g., for processing refunds or handling policy violations).
*   Every manual adjustment requires the admin to provide a mandatory reason.
*   These adjustments are permanently logged in your transaction history with the exact descriptions: **"Added by Marketplace: [Reason]"** or **"Removed by Marketplace: [Reason]"**.

### Unified Transaction Ledger
*   All coin movements are tracked in a single, immutable transaction history ledger.
*   Whether you earn coins from a referral, top up your wallet with Naira, redeem a reward, or receive an admin adjustment, it all appears in one place.
*   This ledger is visible to both you (on your Finance page) and the platform admin (in the Admin Economy panel), ensuring complete transparency and auditability.

*Customer-to-merchant payments do not happen on Marketplace. Customers pay you directly, using the bank details on your invoice or by arrangement in WhatsApp. Marketplace takes no cut.*

| 1 | Customer | Browses, picks options and adds items to the bag. |

| 3 | System | Records the order (it appears under your Leads/Orders) and opens WhatsApp with the itemized message. |
| 4 | Merchant | Receives the order in WhatsApp. Confirms stock and total with the customer. |
| 5 | Merchant | Sends the Invoice link or PDF so the customer knows where to pay. |
| 6 | Customer | Pays the merchant directly. |
| 7 | Merchant | Sets the order to ✅ Paid. The Official Receipt button unlocks. |
| 8 | Merchant | Updates to 🚚 Shipped and then 🏁 Completed, and shares the receipt. |
| 9 | Merchant | If something goes wrong, sets ❌ Cancelled or records a void or refund (these appear in the Audit Ledger). |

*If a customer abandons checkout: They show up in Lost Leads, where you can send a one-tap reminder.*

## 10. Tips for Selling More

**Store setup**
*   Choose the category closest to your business so the AI picks the right style.
*   Write a clear AI prompt: mention colours, mood and customers (for example "dark smokehouse look with ember-orange accents").
*   Upload a clean logo and a wide hero image.

**Products**
*   Use bright, well-lit photos on a plain background.
*   Put key details (size, weight, flavour, delivery time) in the description.
*   Use variants for sizes, colours and portions so customers don't have to message you to ask.
*   Use Always in Stock for food, services and digital items. Use Fixed Stock Count for physical goods.
*   Use Flash Deals sparingly to create urgency.

**Daily routine**
*   Check new orders and update statuses.
*   Open Lost Leads and nudge abandoned carts.
*   Make a flyer for today's best item and post it on your Status.
*   Share your store link in every bio and broadcast.

**Trust**
*   Reply quickly on WhatsApp.
*   Send an invoice, then a receipt, every time. It looks professional and prevents disputes.
*   Turn on public visit stats only once your numbers look healthy.
*   Keep your monthly subscription active so customers never see a locked store!

## 12. Safety Tips

**For merchants**
*   Never share your password, including with anyone claiming to be "support". Platform staff will never ask for it.
*   Use a strong, unique password and keep your email account secure.
*   Log out on shared devices and skip "Remember session" there.
*   Double-check a payment has actually landed in your account before marking an order Paid or sending goods.
*   Keep your Ticket ID private until your account recovery is complete.

**For customers**
*   Check the store name and link before paying anyone.
*   Confirm the merchant's account name matches the store before sending money.
*   Be careful of deals that look too good to be true or pressure you to pay immediately.
*   Keep your invoice and receipt as proof of purchase.

## 14. Under the Hood (Brief)

For the curious:
*   **Language and framework:** Python with the Flask web framework.
*   **Look and feel:** HTML pages styled with Tailwind CSS, with JavaScript for interactive parts such as the bag and flyer studio.
*   **Payments:** Paystack handles monthly subscription payments and wallet top-ups.
*   **Images:** Stored on Cloudinary.
*   **Sign-in:** Email and password, or Google sign-in.
*   **AI:** Store designs and flyers are generated by AI, with heavy jobs handled by a separate helper service so the main site stays fast.
*   **Hosting:** Deployed on Vercel.
*   **Extras:** PDF and image exports happen in your browser.
