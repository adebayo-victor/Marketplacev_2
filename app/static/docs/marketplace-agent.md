## 📌 Executive Overview
Marketplace enables social commerce vendors to launch a professional online storefront in under 60 seconds, streamlining order management and marketing asset creation. It also features a dedicated **Central Campus Hub**, an exclusive, student-to-student marketplace.
* **For Merchants:** Set up instantly, receive structured WhatsApp orders, design promo flyers, issue invoices/receipts, and tap into the student market with 0% commission.
* **For Customers:** Shop freely with no account creation required.
* **For Students:** Verify your student status to access the Central Campus Hub, browse products from fellow students, use a seamless shopping cart, and checkout in one click.

---

## 🛠️ Core Features & Capabilities
* **Custom AI Website Builder:** Deploy vibe-matched storefront layouts rapidly by entering your business description.
* **Direct WhatsApp Ordering:** Orders arrive in your WhatsApp DM with clean, itemized breakdowns.
* **🎓 Central Campus Hub:** An exclusive, verified-student-only marketplace with smart university filtering, a floating shopping cart, Quick View overlays, and bulk checkout that automatically splits orders by merchant.
* **1-Click Status Flyer Studio:** Generate high-resolution PNG & PDF promotional flyers for social statuses using 12 brand themes or AI-generated designs.
* **Invoices & Receipts:** Create formal invoices and stamp them "PAID IN FULL" upon payment verification across 4 professional themes.
* **Made-to-Order Stock Mode & Lead Recovery:** Keep items available for food/service vendors and track cart abandonments to nudge potential buyers.
* **Advanced Merchant Dashboard:** Deep search, status filtering, and source filtering (to easily track Central Hub orders and their associated coin rewards).

## 1. What Marketplace Is
Marketplace is a web platform where a merchant opens one or more kiosks (online stores). Each kiosk gets its own public link, a product catalog, and a WhatsApp-based checkout. Additionally, verified students can opt into the **Central Campus Hub**, a unified marketplace to buy and sell exclusively with other students.

## 7. Page-by-Page Guide: The Central Campus Hub (Students Only)
*(New Section)*
Accessible only to logged-in users with "Student Mode" activated and a valid WhatsApp phone number saved in their settings.

### 7.1 Hub Header & Smart Filter
* **Smart Default Filter:** The campus dropdown automatically defaults to *your* registered university, showing you relevant products immediately. You can change this to "All Universities" or select another campus to browse.

### 7.2 Product Grid & Quick View
* **Product Cards:** Show the product image, merchant name, title, and price.
* **Quick View Modal:** Clicking the product image or title opens a sleek overlay. This shows a larger image, full description, merchant name, price, and a prominent **"Add to Cart"** button, allowing fast browsing without page reloads.

### 7.3 Floating Cart & Bulk Checkout
* **Floating Cart Button:** A persistent button in the bottom-right corner shows the number of items in your cart.
* **Cart Drawer:** Clicking the button slides out a drawer where you can review items, adjust quantities (+/-), or remove items.
* **Bulk Checkout:** When you click "Proceed to Checkout", the system intelligently **splits your cart by merchant**. It creates separate, individual orders for each merchant in the background, so each seller gets their own clean order notification and qualifies for their specific coin rewards.

### 7.4 Pagination
* To ensure lightning-fast loading, the Hub displays 12 products per page. Use the **"Newer"** and **"Older"** buttons at the bottom to navigate through the catalog.

## 9. Coins, Payments, and the Monthly Subscription
*(Add this subsection)*
### Two-Tier Coin Rewards
Marketplace incentivizes campus commerce. When a merchant marks an order as **Paid** or **Completed**:
* **Regular WhatsApp Orders:** Earn the standard sale reward (e.g., 50 coins).
* **🎓 Central Hub Orders:** Earn an elevated bonus reward (e.g., 200 coins) to encourage participation in the student marketplace. This is logged clearly in your Transaction History.

## 10. Order Lifecycle: From Click to Receipt
*(Add this new flow for the Hub)*
| Step | Who | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Student | Browses the Central Hub, uses Quick View, and adds items from multiple merchants to the Floating Cart. |
| 2 | Student | Clicks "Proceed to Checkout". The system splits the cart and creates separate orders for each merchant. |
| 3 | System | Records the orders with `checkout_method = 'central_hub'` and attaches the student's real WhatsApp number. |
| 4 | Merchant | Sees the new order in the Dashboard with a **🎓 Central Hub** badge. Clicks the WhatsApp link to confirm details. |
| 5 | Merchant | Receives payment directly from the student. |
| 6 | Merchant | Sets the order to ✅ Paid. The system **automatically credits the bonus Central Hub coin reward** to the merchant's wallet. |

## 12. Troubleshooting and FAQ
*(Add these new rows to the table)*
| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| **I can't access the Central Campus Hub.** | Ensure you have toggled "Student Mode" ON in Settings, selected a University, and saved a valid WhatsApp Phone Number. |
| **My product isn't showing up in the Central Hub.** | Go to your Inventory, edit the product, and ensure the "🎓 Show in Central Campus Hub" checkbox is ticked. Also, ensure your kiosk is Live. |
| **The merchant sees "Student Account" instead of my number.** | You likely placed the order before adding your phone number. Go to ⚙️ Settings, add your WhatsApp number, and place a new test order. |
| **The mobile menu or tables look squished.** | The app is fully responsive. On mobile, use the hamburger menu (☰) for navigation, and swipe tables horizontally to see all columns. |
